1. Introduction
$PUHU is a Solana-based community token powering a growing Web3 ecosystem built around gaming, community participation and on-chain commerce.
PUHU started as a fair-launch memecoin with a simple philosophy: build without empty promises, keep the community at the center, and create real reasons for people to participate.
Today, the ecosystem is expanding beyond the token itself through Owl Raid, a competitive Web3 gaming platform, and Givedrop, a community interaction and rewards platform.
The long-term goal is to transform an existing Solana community into a persistent ecosystem where users can:
- Play
- Compete
- Earn in-game items
- Trade with other players
- Participate in community events
- Join guilds
- Discover new utilities

6. Player-Driven Economy
One of the core components of Owl Raid is its player-driven marketplace.
Players can list eligible in-game items for other players to purchase using USDC on Solana.
The transaction is executed on-chain between the participating wallets while the item remains part of the Owl Raid game ecosystem.
This creates an economic layer around gameplay:
PLAY → EARN LOOT → TRADE → UPGRADE → COMPETE → WIN → PLAY AGAIN
The objective is to make items and progression meaningful within the game rather than simply creating a token with no product interaction.

7. PvP Arena
The PvP Arena provides a competitive environment where players can compete using their characters and equipment.
Arena matchmaking uses Combat Power, including equipped gear, to determine suitable opponents.
The system is designed around:
- Character statistics
- Equipment
- Combat Power
- Competitive rankings
- Daily fights
- Additional fight boosts
- Player progression
The Arena will continue to evolve toward larger competitive events and tournament-based gameplay.

8. World Boss
Owl Raid includes a persistent World Boss system.
Players can join active World Boss events and contribute damage to the shared boss.
The system uses server-authoritative calculations and records player participation, damage and rankings.
The current World Boss is the Ancient Owl Lord, with different phases and an enrage mechanic.
Future iterations will expand World Boss encounters with additional rewards, competitive rankings and community events.

13. Buyback & Burn
PUHU has a long-term deflationary objective.
A portion of ecosystem revenue may be allocated toward $PUHU market buybacks and permanent token burns, subject to the project's operational requirements and available revenue.
The objective is to create a mechanism through which successful ecosystem products can contribute to reducing the circulating supply of $PUHU.
Buybacks and burns should be considered an ecosystem mechanism and objective, not a guarantee of token appreciation or returns.
